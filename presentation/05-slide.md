# Safety filter: harm before relevance ⏱ 1.5 min

- **Red flag → block conflicting tips + pin "get checked"** — not the whole feed; escalation stays with chat & calls
- **Diagnosis lock:** no "living with X" until X is confirmed · **Sensitive tips:** always discreet
- **Language:** her language → close language → skip and log the gap — never a video she can't follow

> Say: "Irrelevant wastes a slot. Unsafe breaks trust. So safety is code, not a model's judgment."
