# What the prototype taught me ⏱ 1.5 min

- **Even strong models mis-tag content** — 38 fixes → tags need clinical review
- **Embeddings can't spot duplicates** — health tips all "sound alike" → variety uses tags
- **Content depth limits the feed** — Reema runs out of period tips by day 5; a Tamil user gets 1 tip

> Say: "Two of these are content problems, not ranking problems — and the system makes them visible."
